## WHAT IS ARTHVO

Arthvo is India's first gamified financial awareness universe for young Indians aged 18–35.

Not a fintech app. Not a pension calculator. Not a government scheme portal.

A living, personalized environment where young India learns to understand money — through emotion, identity, game mechanics, and AI — in their own language, their own style, their own pace.

---

## THE NAME

**Arth** — Sanskrit for meaning, purpose, wealth, finance. Every Indian language understands it instantly.

**Vo** — Modern, short. Also means "that person" in Hindi. Identity-pointing.

**Arthvo** = "The one who understands money."

Users don't use Arthvo. They become Arthvo.

---

## THE MISSION

500 million young Indians will retire broke if nobody reaches them.

Only 8% of India's youth workforce under 35 has an active NPS account.

PFRDA, banks, and financial institutions have failed to reach them for 20 years because they built information tools. Nobody built emotional tools.

Arthvo reaches them through emotion first, information second.

## THE AVATAR SYSTEM — EMOTIONAL CORE

This is the real product. Everything else serves this.

The avatar is not a cartoon. It is the user's future self.

### 5 Life Scenarios

| State | Corpus vs Target | Scene | Emotion Triggered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thriving | 100%+ | Arjun at 60, travelling, family, own house | Pride |
| Stable | 70–99% | Comfortable flat, simple happy life | Relief |
| Struggling | 40–69% | Dependent on children, worried face | Shame |
| Critical | 10–39% | Working at 65, exhausted | Fear |
| Zero | 0–9% | Alone, grey, blurred future | Dread |

### Avatar Characters
- **Arjun** — Young male, Pune, logistics job
- **Priya** — Young female, Chennai, IT job
- **Raza** — Young male, Lucknow, gig worker
- **Meera** — Young female, Jaipur, student

Same 5 life states for each character. User picks their avatar in onboarding. Their face. Their world.

### How It Connects To Simulator
- Slider moves up → avatar transitions to better life scene
- Slider moves down → avatar transitions to worse life scene
- BhavishyaMitra speaks in corner of scene
- Future self voice: "Yaar please shuru kar. Main theek nahi hoon."
- All transitions via Framer Motion

## INDIAN GAMIFICATION PSYCHOLOGY

Not Duolingo mechanics with Indian skin. Indian psychology driving mechanics.

### Indian Competitive Triggers
- "Tera dost Rahul ne aaj ₹100 bachaya" — peer pressure as fuel
- "Tumhare batch mein top 10 mein ho" — cohort rank
- Family mode — share avatar with parents, they see your progress
- "Tere 847 log dekh rahe hain" — social proof

### Quest Framing
Not "Learn about NPS Tier I"
Instead "Boss Fight: Defeat the Inflation Monster"
Same content. Game wrapper. Indian context.

### Streak System — Made Indian
- Missing a day: "Kal wapas aa. Arjun tera wait kar raha hai." — Invitation, not shame.
- Grace period 4 hours after midnight — for night shift workers and gig workers

### Life Events Quests
- "Ghar se pehli baar kamai" — first salary quest
- "Shaadi ki planning?" — what does marriage cost your retirement?
- "Gig worker ho?" — NPS for self-employed
